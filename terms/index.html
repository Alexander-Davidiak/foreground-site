Your photos

Your photos stay yours, and we never receive them. Foreground can show any photo in the library you give it access to, including ones that are hard to see; you can hide photos and days at any time. If you delete a photo from Foreground, it’s deleted from your library after iOS asks you to confirm. It then follows the usual rules of the Photos app, including Recently Deleted.
