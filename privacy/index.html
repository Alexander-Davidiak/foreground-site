Foreground

Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

  • We never receive your photos. There is no account and no Foreground server.
  • We can’t see which apps you pause or what you do in them. iOS keeps that private.
  • To show a place name, Apple Maps receives a photo’s location.
  • Usage data is shared only if you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time.

Who is responsible

Foreground is made by Oleksandr Davydiak, an independent developer, who is the data controller for the information described here. Write to foreground.app.support@gmail.com with any question.

What stays on your iPhone

Foreground works on your device, and we have no access to what it keeps there: prepared moments, the dates and places it uses to organise them, what you hide, your settings and your setup answers, including which app you named. It analyses photos on the device to choose them, for example by image quality and similarity.

Deleting Foreground removes this data from your iPhone. Photo-related files are excluded from device backups; your settings may be included in them.

Permissions

  • Photos, to show you moments from your library. It changes only if you delete a photo yourself.
  • Screen Time, to pause the apps you choose. iOS gives Foreground opaque tokens, not app names or activity.
  • Notifications, if you allow them, for reminders scheduled on the device.

Foreground doesn’t ask for your current location. Change any of these in iPhone Settings.

What leaves your iPhone

Place names (Apple Maps)

Foreground uses Apple Maps to turn a photo’s location into a city and country. For moments in a pause, Apple receives the location saved with the photo, which can be precise. To organise your days, it receives a location rounded to about 10 km. No photo, name or account of ours is sent with it, and we never receive these requests. Photos without a location are not looked up. Apple handles the requests under its own privacy policy.

iCloud Photos

If your library is in iCloud, iOS may download a photo from Apple so it can be shown. This happens between your device and your own iCloud account.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases of Foreground Pro. We don’t receive your payment details or your Apple Account identity. Apple gives us aggregated sales reports. If you share diagnostics with app developers in iOS, Apple may also give us crash and performance reports.

Emails to us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. «Contact us» in the app adds the app version, iPhone model and iOS version to the draft, where you can see or remove them. Our mailbox is hosted by Google. We use these emails only to reply, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Usage data (only if you agree)

When you first open Foreground, it asks whether you’d like to share usage data. Nothing is sent before you agree, and the app works the same either way. If you agree, we use PostHog, an analytics service, to learn whether the pause works and where the app needs fixing.

What is sent:

  • A random ID that connects your events over time. It isn’t tied to your name, email or Apple Account.
  • How the app is used: setup, including your answers to its questions; pauses; limits; purchases; errors.
  • Device model, iOS version and app version.

What is never sent: your photos, their dates, places or identifiers, which apps you pause, anything you type, your name or your email. There is no screen recording.

PostHog stores this data in the EU on our behalf, under a data processing agreement that requires it to protect the data as this policy does. Your IP address reaches PostHog when data is sent, but is not stored with your events. Events are deleted automatically after 12 months, at PostHog’s next monthly cleanup.

To stop: turn off «Share usage data» in Settings › Privacy. Sending stops and the random ID is discarded. Because events aren’t tied to you, we can’t look up a person’s earlier events; they are deleted when the 12 months end.

This website

This site uses no cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell data, show ads or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Your rights

If the GDPR or a similar law applies to you, we rely on your consent for usage data and on our legitimate interest in answering you for emails. You may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or limit its use, to receive a copy, and to complain to your data protection authority. Most data is only on your device, so you control it directly. For anything else, write to us.

Children

Foreground isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has shared data with us, write to us and we’ll delete what we can identify.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above, and note important changes in the App Store release notes.
