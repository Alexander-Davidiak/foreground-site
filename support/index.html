Support

Something not working, or an idea to share? Write to foreground.app.support@gmail.com. Your iPhone model and iOS version help.

The pause doesn’t appear

Open Foreground and check its home screen, which shows when something is missing. Screen Time access for Foreground must be on in iPhone Settings › Screen Time › Apps With Screen Time Access. iOS decides when a pause can be shown, so one can occasionally be late, especially after an update or a restart.

A pause has no photos, or too few

Foreground shows photos only from the library you gave it access to. Change this in iPhone Settings › Apps › Foreground › Photos.

A photo I don’t want to see

You can hide a photo or a whole day, and it won’t come back. Your library stays as it is. Everything you’ve hidden is in Foreground’s Settings.

Get Pro back

Use Restore on the Pro screen, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel or get a refund

Apple manages billing. Cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, and ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Deleting Foreground removes its data from your iPhone. The Privacy Policy explains what stays on the device and what doesn’t.
